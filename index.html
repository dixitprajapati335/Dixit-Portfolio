04 · Explorations

Outside the brief.

Pottery, photography and calligraphy are the hands-on practices that balance my professional work. They sharpen my eye, build patience and keep my process curious. Different materials, one consistent way of thinking.

Your turn at the wheel

Throw a pot.

Clay teaches form, patience and how to respond when the material behaves differently from the plan. Drag across the spinning clay to shape it. Pull outwards to widen, push inwards to narrow.

No Ctrl+Z at the wheel. That's the whole lesson.

06 · About

A designer who asks why first.

I'm Dixit, a designer working across brand strategy, positioning, brand architecture and visual systems. I do my best work where those four meet.

My interest lies in the thinking behind design: why a brand exists, where it sits in its market, how its parts fit together, and how it should look once that is clear. I'm building towards a career in brand strategy and creative direction.

Outside of design projects I practise pottery, photography and calligraphy. These slower, hands-on pursuits keep me patient and sharpen my eye for detail.
